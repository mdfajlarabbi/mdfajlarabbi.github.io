I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Clemson University, where I also serve as a Graduate Laboratory Assistant for ME 4440. My research interests include functional materials, polymer nanocomposites, MEMS, flexible electronics, energy harvesting, and self-powered sensors.

I received my M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Ulsan, South Korea, where I was a graduate research assistant in the Fluid Power & Machine Intelligence (FPMI) Laboratory under Prof. Kyoung Kwan Ahn. My research focused on electrospun TiO2–PVDF nanocomposite membranes for liquid–solid triboelectric nanogenerators. I characterized the membranes using FE-SEM, AFM, XRD, and FTIR, measured device output with a Keithley DMM7510 and an oscilloscope, and modeled device behavior in COMSOL Multiphysics.

Prior to graduate school, I worked for nearly four years as a mechanical project engineer at MPVC Limited on the first VCM plant in Bangladesh, with OxyVinyls as the process licensor and HYEC as the detailed engineering contractor.

Research

Graduate research assistant, FPMI Lab

Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Ulsan. Advisor: Prof. Kyoung Kwan Ahn

  • Fabricated electrospun TiO2-PVDF nanocomposite membranes to optimize β-phase content, surface roughness, and surface charge density for liquid-solid triboelectric nanogenerator (TENG) devices.
  • Characterized β-phase content using XRD (Rigaku Ultima IV) and FTIR (Thermo Fisher Nicolet iS5), morphology using FE-SEM (JEOL JSM-7600) with EDS mapping, and surface roughness using AFM (MFP-3D).
  • Measured membrane hydrophobicity by water contact angle using a SmartDrop analyzer (FemtoFAB), and dielectric constant using an Agilent 4294A precision impedance analyzer.
  • Built droplet-based liquid-solid TENG devices and designed a 3D-printed cascaded structure with two stacked membranes.
  • Measured open-circuit voltage (Voc), short-circuit current (Isc), and transferred charge using a Keithley DMM7510 digital multimeter.
  • Wrote a first-author review on electrospun nanofibers for TENGs (Advanced Materials, 2026).
